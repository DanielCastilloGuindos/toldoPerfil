# Toldo Perfil Capabilities and Features

## What this service can accomplish
We provide customized awning design, assembly, installation, and repairs. Specifically, we can:
- Estimate awning sizes, layout requirements, and hardware constraints.
- Suggest best materials (acrylic, PVC, screen fabrics) based on local weather constraints.
- Provide custom solutions for residential balconies, private terraces, commercial storefronts, and communities.
- Dispatch installation professionals to Velilla de San Antonio, Loeches, and other municipalities in Madrid.

## Required inputs and parameters
To process an awning configuration or quote, we require the following inputs:
- **Location**: Installation municipality (Velilla de San Antonio, Loeches, or Madrid in general).
- **Dimensions**: Desired width (ancho) and projection/drop (salida/línea).
- **Type**: Awning type choice (e.g., monobloc, chest/cofre, articulated arm, capota).
- **Control**: Manual or motorization/automation preference.

## Constraints and limitations
- **Geographic restriction**: Physical installation is limited to the Community of Madrid, focusing on Velilla de San Antonio and Loeches.
- **Structural limits**: Max width for single module is 7 meters; max projection is 4 meters.
- **Wind limits**: Awnings must be retracted when wind speeds exceed 38 km/h (unless equipped with wind/rain auto-sensors).
